WHAT THIS IS
------------
Build one page on the Idea Factory Webflow site: the Office of Business,
Finance, and Facilities page for Idea Factory / REFI staff and students.
It replaces the old Mtech page at https://www.mtech.umd.edu/obff.

The site footer's bottom-right link "Idea Factory / REFI Staff and
Students ››" opens this page (on its Purchasing tab).

Reference: the 6-page visual layout guide PDF (kept at its Dropbox source,
not in this repo, per housekeeping policy — see CLAUDE.md/CLAUDE-HISTORY.md):
https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/crze1ikyljaj9sdxdwz2h/Idea-Factory-site-OBFF-page-layout-guide.pdf?rlkey=jc25ywygm8k0u3h3f3x5axxg4&st=eeut2aj8&dl=0
Page 1 is an index. Pages 2 to 6 each show the SAME page with a different
tab selected and every accordion on that tab opened.

HOW TO READ THE PDF
-------------------
- The yellow band at the top of each PDF page is a label added for you.
  It is not part of the design. It names the tab, its URL, and how many
  accordions it has.
- Small red "ACCORDION · collapsed by default · shown expanded" tags next
  to accordion titles are also labels, not part of the design.
- In the real page every accordion starts CLOSED. The PDF shows them open
  only so you can see their content.
- The PDF was captured at about 920px wide, so it shows the tablet layout.
  Build the normal desktop layout at the site's standard container width,
  using the structure and styling described here, and let it reflow to
  this tablet layout and to a single column on phones.
- The site header and footer are the standard Idea Factory site header and
  footer used on every page. The footer is omitted from the PDF.

PAGE STRUCTURE (top to bottom)
------------------------------
1. Standard site header.
2. Hero band, black background:
   - Gold eyebrow, uppercase, wide letter-spacing:
     "Idea Factory / REFI Staff and Students"
   - Large white heading: "Office of Business, Finance, and Facilities"
   - A row of five tabs directly under the heading (wraps to two rows on
     narrow screens). Selected tab: gold background, black text.
     Unselected tabs: translucent white background, white text. Uppercase
     Interstate, heavy weight.
   - A red bar along the bottom-right edge of the band (about 38% of the
     width).
   This hero is the same on every tab except for which tab is selected.
3. White content area for the selected tab:
   - Tab heading (bold, black), e.g. "University of Maryland's - Idea
     Factory's Purchasing Policy".
   - "Purpose:" paragraph (the word "Purpose:" in red, bold) on Purchasing,
     Travel, and HR. Professional Development has an italic intro
     paragraph instead. Elevate has neither.
   - Then either:
       a) always-visible content under a heavy black rule (Purchasing and
          Elevate), or
       b) a stack of accordions under a heavy black rule (Travel, HR, and
          Professional Development).
4. Standard site footer.

THE FIVE TABS
-------------
  1. Purchasing  ->  #staff-purchasing  ->  no accordions
  2. Travel  ->  #staff-travel  ->  3 accordion(s)
  3. HR Employment & Onboarding  ->  #staff-hr  ->  7 accordion(s)
  4. Elevate  ->  #staff-elevate  ->  no accordions
  5. Professional Development  ->  #staff-development  ->  2 accordion(s)

This is ONE page. The tabs switch which content is shown; they do not go
to different pages. Each tab has its own URL fragment so any tab can be
linked to directly:

  <page URL>#staff-purchasing   (default when no fragment is given)
  <page URL>#staff-travel
  <page URL>#staff-hr
  <page URL>#staff-elevate
  <page URL>#staff-development

Implementation in Webflow: use a Tabs element (or show/hide sections) plus
a small script that (1) on load reads location.hash and selects the
matching tab, defaulting to Purchasing, and (2) when a tab is clicked,
updates location.hash without reloading. Mark the selected tab with
aria-current or aria-selected for accessibility.

Page URL: to be confirmed by the Idea Factory team (for example
https://www.ideafactory.umd.edu/obff, matching the old address). Add a
301 redirect from https://www.mtech.umd.edu/obff.

    - I. University of Maryland (UMD) Resources
    - II. External Resources

Behavior:
- All accordions start closed. Clicking the title row opens or closes it.
- More than one may be open at once.
- Title row: bold black title on the left, a square 30px "+" button with a
  2px black border on the right. When open, the square fills black and
  shows "-" in white.
- Thin gray rule between accordions; heavy black rule above the first.
- Use a <button> with aria-expanded for each title row.

CONTENT STYLING (inside the content area and accordions)
--------------------------------------------------------
- Body text: the site's serif body font, about 17px, 1.65 line height,
  maximum line length about 78 characters.
- Sub-headings inside accordions (e.g. "HERE'S HOW TO GET STARTED:",
  "AIRFARE:", "LODGING:"): Interstate, heavy, uppercase, wide
  letter-spacing, black.
- Bulleted lists with some nested lettered lists (a, b, c).
- Bold run-in labels at the start of list items (e.g. "Purchasing Email:").
- Links and email addresses: site link color (red), underlined. Long URLs
  wrap.
